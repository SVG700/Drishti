# Brag Plan: DRISHTI (दृष्टि)

## What is this app?
DRISHTI is an AI-powered national infrastructure monitoring and intelligence platform engineered for the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) PAIMANA initiative (Problem Statement SIH26103) by Team KAALNETRA. It transforms 11,000+ monthly flash report observations across 2,100 Central Sector projects worth ₹45+ Lakh Crores into real-time geospatial intelligence, predictive delay forecasting, and grounded AI assistant briefings.

## The angle
High-stakes national command center intelligence. Not another generic CRUD dashboard, but a planetary-scale surveillance and predictive analytics nexus that empowers senior decision-makers to prevent multi-thousand-crore project slippages through real 3D spatial mapping and empirical Bayesian run-rate forecasts.

- **Executive Dashboard (8–20s)**: High-level command telemetry displaying real figures from the live platform — 2,100 total undertakings, 1,397 delayed projects, ₹4,534,196 Cr portfolio cost, and sector capital distribution led by Roads & Highways and Railways.
- **Projects Explorer & Dossier (20–32s)**: Live filtering across 2,100 official projects and seamless opening of the Project Dossier for #400102 (*Sri Ram Sagar Project Stage - II*), revealing exact sanctioned budgets, 9-month snapshot histories, and 72-month time overrun data.
